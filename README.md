# Nobody Fools Archie Fairweather
*An Archie Fairweather Mystery — Book 1*

A cozy mystery. Merrow, Maine, Halloween week. The world's most famous magician, the medium who dies in his theater, and the one trick nobody else could see.

## Layout
- `foundation/` — north star, character cards (full + drafting-redacted), bible, mystery spine (planner-only), outline & chapter briefs, style guide
- `craft/` — the craft standard (imported from the A Manuscript of Lies project; Penn-series-specific rules adapted in `foundation/style-guide.md`)
- `manuscript/` — chapters
- `reviews/` — red-team, panel and editorial reports
- **`Nobody-Fools-Archie-Fairweather.md` — the complete novel (~80,000 words, 44 chapters)**
- `compile.py` — rebuilds the compiled manuscript from `manuscript/`

## Status
Complete. Drafted, red-teamed, revised twice from six blind reader reports plus Story Grid, craft and continuity audits, line-edited and proofread. Final blind reader: 4.5★, catharsis 9/10, would buy Book 2 on release day 9/10. See `reviews/` for every report and log.
