# Outline — Nobody Fools Archie Fairweather

## Shape
| Act | Chapters | Days | Movement |
|---|---|---|---|
| I — The Wager | 1–9 | Thu 23 – Sat 25 Oct | A medium comes to Archie's theater; a wager; a séance; a death |
| IIA — The Impossible | 10–22 | Sun 26 – Tue 28 | Archie's trumpet theory vs. the police's tea theory; Archie suspected; Archie wrong about Warren; **Midpoint (22): Archie proved right — and his truth jails Ivy** |
| IIB — The Wrong Woman | 23–33 | Tue 28 – Fri 31 (5 a.m.) | Clearing Ivy narrows the window onto Archie; cut off; the town rallies; June backstage; the ledgers; **the click (32–33)** |
| III — The Empty Chair | 34–44 | Fri 31 – Sat 1 Nov | Tess's route; Hollis; the show; crisis; the bell buoy; the chair; restoration; the trunk |

Global value per act (justice / performance↔presence): I: hidden injustice → apparent tragedy / performance (Archie dazzles, keeps everyone in the audience). IIA: suspicion → injustice (an innocent jailed on true evidence) / performance costs others (Warren, Shannon). IIB: injustice wearing justice's authority → truth found / Archie lets June backstage. III: justice with one cost / presence (he comes back).

## ACT I — THE WAGER

### Sequence 1 — "The Medium Comes to Merrow" (Ch 1–4). Thursday–Friday.
*Sequence turn:* Archie goes from delighted owner of a crumbling theater to a man who has bet two million dollars, his grandfather's memory and his mother's heart against a woman who knows something she shouldn't.

### Ch 1 — Upside Down
**Thu Oct 23, ~10 a.m. · The Merrow Opera House (stage, fly gallery) · Archie, Lenny; Hollis leading Lorelei, Ivy, Teddy on a walkthrough · small group, comic → charged.**
**Orientation must establish:** Merrow, Maine, late October; the half-restored opera house (cold, radiators knocking, the 1899 painted harbor drape); who Archie is (world's most famous magician, vanished from his own Vegas show last spring, now here) — via action and others' reactions, not a résumé block.
**Pursuit:** Archie, hanging upside down in a straitjacket from the fly gallery with Lenny on the rope, is trying to beat his grandfather's escape time for the Halloween tribute show. He is having the time of his life.
**Counterpressure:** Hollis brings in the TV people early. Lorelei wants him rattled and on camera (Teddy wants the feud; it's ratings). She treats him, upside down, as a delightful specimen.
**Development:** Archie tries to dismiss her (patter, debunking jabs, from upside down) → she agrees to his "controls" with suspicious ease, then cold-reads him and lands something about his grandfather's trunk — that he still hasn't opened it → Archie, shaken, escapes in a record time on pure adrenaline, lands on his feet, and turns the moment into a challenge: his standing million-dollar Fairweather Challenge, on her séance, his rules. Lorelei raises: two million, and he sits *in* the circle. He accepts before he thinks.
**Required facts:** Hollis wrote to the TV company in May, before Archie came, when the town needed a new roof ($40,000 location fee) — said plainly, she's unrepentant and practical. The séance is this Saturday night, taped for a Halloween special. Ivy present, silent, watchful; Teddy on his phone.
**Value:** control → loss of control (internal: the performer is seen).
**Exit:** He's agreed to sit in a séance on Saturday — and someone told her about the trunk.

### Ch 2 — The Trunk
**Thu Oct 23, evening · Fairweather House kitchen → turret · Archie, June, Vivienne; Lenny passing through · family.**
**Orientation:** the house (Gull Point, turret, Gus's trapdoors and revolving bookcase) seen through a family dinner in progress.
**Pursuit:** Archie wants a triumphant dinner (he's attempting live lobster and cannot bring himself to cook it — it has a name by the end) and wants to keep the séance from upsetting his mother.
**Counterpressure:** Vivienne already knows — the whole town knows — and announces she will sit in the séance. June wants the circus to stop; she's been the adult since she was nine and he promised her a normal year.
**Development:** Archie forbids → Vivienne overrules him (calls him Archibald) → June cross-examines him about the two-million-dollar bet → he promises no circus (he means it; he can't keep it). Later, in the turret, Archie at the trunk with his notebook of thousands of failed words. Vivienne joins him: "He said when he's gone." Archie asks how a stranger could know about the trunk; Vivienne: everyone in Merrow knows — it was in the paper in 1988. Deflation, and unease that it's not enough.
**Required facts:** the trunk (seven letter-dials; Gus's card on the lid; the town story that forcing it ruins the contents). Archie hangs Gus's opera cloak for the show and folds it a particular way without thinking; Vivienne: it's exactly how her father did it. Gus vanished Halloween 1988; Archie was ten, in the second row.
**Pleasures (optional):** the lobster; June's homework at the table; Lenny's refusal to eat anything from the sea.
**Exit:** 1 a.m., Archie spinning dials. Nothing. How did she know?

### Ch 3 — The Liars' Table
**Fri Oct 24, 6:30 a.m. · The Galley → the end of the town wharf · Archie, Bev, Shannon (waitressing), old lobstermen at the liars' table, Hollis, Warren, Chief Pinkham · full room → one-to-one (two connected scenes).**
**Pursuit:** Archie wants the lobstermen to accept him (he wants to go out hauling; they ignore him). Then the room wants the gossip: how do mediums fake it?
**Counterpressure:** Bev refuses to be impressed. Warren arrives and wants Archie to know the opera-house sale doesn't close till November 15 and the town won't be made a laughingstock. Shannon, pouring coffee, wants everyone to stop laughing.
**Development:** group-scene engine — Archie demonstrates séance tricks with salt shakers, a sugar spoon and Bev's hand (the room joins, heckles, plays); someone asks about the trumpet; Hollis, from her booth, supplies the page in Gus's book and the detail (lips to the small end) for a laugh → Warren needles; Archie needles back and wins the room, which doesn't help → Archie notices Shannon flinch and leave. He follows her onto the wharf, to the fishermen's memorial, where her husband's name is fresh-cut at the bottom. She's a sitter. Lorelei's been "talking to Tommy" for her; she has paid. "Don't you dare make fun of it." Archie, sincerity drop, promises he won't humiliate her.
**Required facts:** the memorial — a few names above Tommy's, read in passing, include *Peter Langley 2003* (one ordinary list; attention on Tommy). Hollis's page-112 line is a laugh. Shannon has two kids; Tommy lost off Monhegan in February.
**Value:** hidden injustice surfaces (Shannon is being worked).
**Exit:** his crusade now has a person in it.

### Ch 4 — Sea View
**Fri Oct 24, ~2 p.m. · Sea View memory care, common room · Archie, Walt Calloway, residents (incl. a small woman named Carol who keeps asking when her husband is coming), aide; Tess arrives · small crowd → two.**
**Orientation:** Archie has been coming here every Friday since September, unannounced, no cameras. This is the realest he is.
**Pursuit:** make the residents laugh; make Walt remember something good. Walt does: Gus, vividly. "Gus Fairweather still owes me a boat." (Just the line; Archie laughs it off.)
**Counterpressure:** Tess arrives for her visit and sees a celebrity using her father — she assumes a stunt and moves to end it. She wants her father's dignity protected and this man gone.
**Development:** she confronts him; he's charming; it doesn't work → she checks for a camera; there isn't one → he performs for Walt: **lift #1** — Archie lifts Tess's pen and produces it from Walt's cardigan pocket; Walt laughs, a real laugh, the first in months; Tess sees it → she softens one degree and hardens two. She knows about the séance (everyone does): "Don't make it a circus." He's already making it a circus. She gives him her card "for when someone gets hurt."
**Required facts:** Tess is MSP Major Crimes, grew up here; Walt was chief for thirty-two years; Walt sometimes thinks Tess is fifteen. Carol (unnamed surname) present as texture.
**Value:** contempt → curiosity (relationship). Internal: Archie present, not performing — and she can't see it yet.
**Exit:** Walt, fondly: that's his daughter; she's fifteen. Archie: she's terrifying. Walt: ayuh.

### Sequence 2 — "The Séance" (Ch 5–9). Friday–Saturday night.
*Sequence turn:* from rivalry to recognition to death. Archie goes in to unmask a fraud and comes out holding a dying woman.

### Ch 5 — The Sign-Out Card
**Fri Oct 24, ~4:30 p.m. · Merrow Public Library · Archie, Deb Coombs (librarian), June · small, intimate.**
**Pursuit:** Archie wants Gus's own book, *The Dead Don't Whisper*, to refresh the séance chapter before Saturday.
**Counterpressure:** Deb guards the Fairweather Collection like a dragon: in-library use only, she doesn't care whose grandson he is.
**Development:** charm fails → bargain (something specific he offers the library — optional: a reading-room radiator; a children's show) partly works; Deb brings it to a reading table → the sign-out card in its pocket: a handful of names over forty years — *W. Fairweather* → Archie finds June in the reading-room corner with a deck of cards under her math homework. She's been teaching herself from Gus's books. She's good. He's overjoyed; she's mortified → he oversteps (the big gesture: she'll do the show with him!) → she shuts him down: it's hers, not his.
**Required facts:** the card names as above, presented as a charming relic; attention on Walt's and June's names. Page 112 (the trumpet) — Archie reads it briefly.
**Value:** internal: spectacle-love refused; June has a secret life in magic.
**Exit:** he has to let it be hers. He doesn't know how.

### Ch 6 — Two of a Kind
**Fri Oct 24, ~8 p.m. · Spindrift Inn, fireside parlor · Archie, Lorelei; Ivy and Teddy at the edges · one-to-one with interruptions.**
**Pursuit:** Archie wants airtight controls (sit beside her, search her, his own cameras).
**Counterpressure:** Lorelei wants him in the circle and on camera — and is genuinely curious about him. She concedes small things and refuses the ones that matter, laughing.
**Development:** bargaining as sparring → she cold-reads him again; he reads her back and lands it (she's tired; part of her wants to be caught) → a moment of real recognition between two performers; she says this is her last season → Ivy appears in the doorway; a cold mother–daughter exchange → leaving, Archie overhears Ivy in the parking lot: after this one she's done; Lorelei: she'll be done when Lorelei says → Teddy on the phone in the hall, a fragment about the key-person policy.
**Required facts:** Lorelei mentions doing church halls in Maine "back in the day" — they all blur (texture). Her rings, her lipstick, her laugh. She should be likable enough to mourn.
**Value:** rivalry → recognition.
**Exit:** Archie drives home in the hearse unsettled because he likes her.

### Ch 7 — Controls
**Sat Oct 25, ~6:45–7:58 p.m. · The opera house: stage, wings, the hall · Everyone: Archie, Vivienne, Shannon, Warren, Kev; Lorelei, Ivy; Teddy and his crew (Marco on a handheld "B-roll" camera); Hollis; Chief Pinkham at the back · full-room set-piece (approved: one continuous set-piece; ≤2,300 words).**
**Orientation:** the transformed hall — round oak table center stage, seven chairs, candles, two infrared cameras on tripods, cables, the painted drape glowing, cold.
**Pursuit:** Archie wants his controls airtight, on camera.
**Counterpressure:** Lorelei keeps winning small points; Teddy wants a show; Kev (starstruck, the contest-winner superfan with his own logo hoodie) wants autographs from both.
**Development (play the human interactions):** the controls segment on the official camera: Archie inspects the table and props; picks up the trumpet, looks through it, and hams it for the camera — lips to the small end, a raspberry toot, "no spirits in here" → he asks to search Lorelei; refused; he loses the point → seating fight: he wants the chair beside her; refused ("hostile energy"); Ivy at her left is non-negotiable; Archie gets to choose her right-hand sitter and chooses Shannon → Lorelei murmurs something to Warren; Warren goes white → the tea truce: Archie pours chamomile from Bev's dented thermos for himself and Lorelei; they toast on camera → Lorelei and Ivy go down to the green room → Teddy tells Kev to put his phone away → Archie at the back with Chief Pinkham, who wants to talk about the riverboat → bustle at the table: Hollis, in black stage gloves, setting water glasses; Teddy calls to her to slide "the horn" left, out of the candle's way, and she does (one sentence, in among Vivienne arranging her shawl and Shannon twisting her ring) → Archie and Vivienne, a private moment: neither has to do this → Lorelei returns; candles; hands.
**Required facts:** all of the above at ordinary emphasis; the seating order (from Lorelei's left: Ivy, Warren, Vivienne, Archie opposite her, Kev, Shannon at her right).
**Exit:** the house lights go out.

### Ch 8 — The Other Side
**Sat Oct 25, 8:00–8:31 p.m. · The opera house stage, in darkness · the seven sitters; Hollis's voice from the booth at the end; Teddy and crew unseen · the circle (approved: second consecutive opera-house chapter — cast and register invert from bustle to dark intimacy).**
**Pursuit:** Archie, holding his mother's hand and Kev's, wants to catch every trick in the act and say so afterward.
**Counterpressure:** Lorelei's performance is superb; Vivienne's grip says she wants it to be real; the dark itself.
**Development:** red lamp, prayer, "Brother Amos"; raps; the bell; cold touches (Warren yelps, Vivienne laughs); the infrared "static" that her fans call the Crane Static; Archie silently naming methods, wry → the luminous trumpet rises and floats → "Tommy" for Shannon: it's warm here, the water's warm, he's not cold anymore; Shannon sobbing; Archie's fury → the voice changes: Gus. "Viv." Then "Sparrow" — a name Archie had forgotten anyone ever called him; for one second he wants it to be real → "the trunk, Sparrow — the answer's in the —" the voice thickens, coughs → the trumpet falls; Lorelei, in her own voice: her mouth, she can't feel her mouth → Archie shouts for lights, for Hollis → lights: Lorelei gray and sweating; Ivy holding her left hand, Shannon her right → Archie over the table to her → from the floor he sees Ivy pick up the trumpet and wipe its small end with her silk scarf and push the scarf into her coat → Lorelei's last words to him (a performer's question; drafter's choice) → Chief Pinkham on the radio.
**Required facts:** every item above, physically clear. No gore. The death itself is not on the page — she's alive when the chapter ends.
**Value:** life → mortal danger (justice: the inciting incident).
**Exit:** Lorelei's eyes on Archie as the EMTs come up the stairs.

### Ch 9 — The Waiting Room
**Sat Oct 25, ~9:00–10:45 p.m. · ER waiting room, the hospital in Rockport · Archie, Ivy; Dr. Rao; Tess arrives · small, fluorescent, quiet.**
**Pursuit:** Archie drove Ivy behind the ambulance in the hearse because no one else would (Teddy's on the phone to the network). He wants to look after her — and can't stop himself wanting to ask about the free hand.
**Counterpressure:** Ivy wants nothing from him; she sits with her mother's handbag on her knees and lies: "She held my hand."
**Development:** small kindnesses (vending-machine tea; his coat) → the question slips out; she shuts down → Dr. Rao: Lorelei died at 9:40 → Ivy's terrible stillness → Tess arrives (Pinkham called MSP; she's on call and local) → Archie, privately, tells her: the numb mouth → aconite (wolfsbane; he once built a whole Borgia routine and read everything); the trumpet; he saw Ivy wipe it. Honest crisis played small: saying it costs Ivy; not saying it is a lie. He says it. → Tess: how does he know about aconite? She phones Pinkham to lock the opera house and bag the trumpet.
**Required facts:** time of death 9:40; Archie names aconite before anyone else; he tells Tess about the wipe; Ivy sees him talking to the detective.
**Value:** uncertainty → death; Archie chooses truth at someone else's cost (first instance).
**Exit:** Archie realizes he's just made himself the most interesting man in the room to the State Police.

## ACT IIA — THE IMPOSSIBLE

### Sequence 3 — "Sunday" (Ch 10–14)
*Sequence turn:* Archie commits to finding out how she died — and learns that every truth he finds costs someone.

### Ch 10 — Pancakes
**Sun Oct 26, ~7 a.m. · Fairweather House kitchen · Archie, June, Vivienne, Lenny · family.**
**Pursuit:** after no sleep, Archie wants to make it a normal Sunday (pancakes shaped like playing cards — they don't hold their shape) and to protect his mother.
**Counterpressure:** Vivienne is fragile and won't be protected: she heard "Sparrow" too; her father called him that; she'd forgotten; how could that woman know? June wants the truth, not a show, and debates him.
**Development:** Lenny, practical, explains how a trumpet floats (a rod or a free hand) → Archie's theory: the trumpet, not the tea → June takes it apart like a debate: if he's right, someone knew she was faking and knew she'd put her mouth on it — that's a short list, and he's on it → Archie commits: he's going to find out (appetite; Vivienne's pain; that name). On the way out, Vivienne reminds him Bev's thermos is still in the hearse; he'll drop it at The Galley.
**Value:** shock → commitment. Internal: performing cheer vs. a daughter who wants the real thing.
**Exit:** Tess calls. Can he come to the opera house. Bring his expertise.

### Ch 11 — Walkthrough
**Sun Oct 26, ~10 a.m. · The opera house (taped scene) · Archie, Tess, Chief Pinkham, Officer Kayla Bissonnette, a crime-scene tech; Hollis with the keys · small working group.**
**Pursuit:** Archie wants to prove the trumpet theory and find the switch that killed the cameras.
**Counterpressure:** Tess wants his expertise on how a séance is faked — without letting him take over or touch anything. Every leap he makes, she asks for the evidence.
**Development:** he reconstructs the circle with live volunteers (Pinkham in Ivy's chair, Kayla in Lorelei's) — the room joins the game → the hand release; how the trumpet floats → Tess: "could" isn't "did" → Hollis, unlocking and turning on the radiators, answers routine questions: she moved the horn when Teddy asked; and Warren borrowed her prop-room key Friday afternoon "for the inventory" (this second detail catches Archie) → Archie, reasoning about where Ivy would ditch a switch on the way to the ambulance, finds a small black remote in the stairwell umbrella stand → triumph → Tess bags it, irritated he touched it.
**Value:** death → possible murder. Partnership ↔ suspicion.
**Exit:** Tess: the autopsy's this afternoon; she's asked for his wolfsbane. Aconite, he corrects. If it's positive, she says, he understands what that means for him.

### Ch 12 — The Captain's Suite
**Sun Oct 26, ~1 p.m. · Spindrift Inn, Lorelei's suite · Archie, Ivy; Teddy intrudes · one-to-one.**
**Pursuit:** Archie brings Ivy chowder from The Galley and wants her to admit the free hand.
**Counterpressure:** Ivy wants her mother's legacy intact, him gone, and to stop Teddy airing the death.
**Development:** kindness → she takes the chowder, gives nothing → his read of her (she's been running the static since she was a kid) → she freezes him out → he tells her he saw the wipe and understands it — a reset, he'd have done the same → she cracks only about her mother: Lorelei never told *her* it was the last season → Teddy bursts in wanting Ivy's sign-off to air the special "as she'd have wanted"; Ivy throws him out; Archie helps; Ivy: don't help me.
**Required facts:** Ivy's research binder on the desk with tabs for each sitter — Archie glimpses names; she puts it in a drawer.
**Value:** empathy ≠ truth.
**Exit:** "She held my hand, Mr. Fairweather. Write that down."

### Ch 13 — Rosie and Liam
**Sun Oct 26, ~4 p.m. · Shannon Mayo's small house near the co-op · Archie, Shannon, Liam (7), Rosie (4); Hollis arrives · domestic.**
**Pursuit:** Archie wants to check on Shannon — and to ask if Lorelei's grip on her right hand ever changed.
**Counterpressure:** Shannon wants Tommy's voice back and blames Archie for taking it.
**Development:** she won't let him in → the kids do (Liam knows who he is) → a trick for the kids that delights them and makes Shannon angrier → she lets him have it: did he get what he wanted? → then, flatly, she answers his question: Lorelei never let go of her right hand; held so hard her rings hurt; she'd paid $1,050; Lorelei promised "something special" at the séance → Hollis arrives with a casserole (she's Rosie's godmother), firm and warm, and sends Archie home: he's done enough good for one weekend.
**Value:** comfort ↔ truth: Archie's crusade costs Shannon.
**Exit:** on the step, Hollis: Shannon will forgive him. Not this week.

### Ch 14 — Five-Card Draw
**Sun Oct 26, ~8 p.m. · Fairweather House dining room · Archie, Bev, Chief Pinkham, Ozzie, Lenny, Vivienne; June on the stairs; Tess arrives late · group of seven.**
**Pursuit:** Sunday poker is a standing game Archie started in September; he wants a normal night and the town's gossip.
**Counterpressure:** the town wants to play cards and not talk about it (until they do); Bev wants his money.
**Development:** Archie can't bluff and won't cheat; Bev wins → the gossip: the State Police lieutenant is coming from Augusta tomorrow; and Bev, mentioning his thermos, says she washed it — she washes everything that comes back → Archie's stomach drops; the table jokes; he doesn't → Tess at the door: autopsy done, tox pending; he's been to see Ivy and Shannon; stop → Vivienne makes her sit; one hand; Tess reads his tell and takes his chips.
**Pleasures:** genuine — Ozzie and Vivienne, June watching from the stairs, Lenny's silence, Bev's ruthlessness.
**Value:** normal → uneasy; friction → warmth.
**Exit:** on the porch: why did she come herself? To see his face when she told him to stop. And? He's not going to stop.

### Sequence 4 — "Person of Interest" (Ch 15–22)
*Sequence turn:* the poison is confirmed, Archie is suspected, he's wrong about Warren at real cost — and then he's proved right, and his rightness puts Ivy in jail.

### Ch 15 — Hauling

**Development:** dawn on the water (Merrow Light, the bell buoy off Shag Ledge, cold) → Archie tries to haul, does one trap well, then is seasick over the side → Dale, by the bell buoy, tells the story of Gus's skiff found drifting here in '88 → Dale's phone: preliminary tox positive for aconite; it's a murder inquiry → Dale, mortified, tells him anyway because he believes him.
**Value:** pleasure → dread.
**Exit:** the lieutenant wants him at two. Bring a lawyer. He doesn't need a lawyer. Ayuh — bring one anyway.

### Ch 16 — Person of Interest
**Mon Oct 27, 2 p.m. · The select board room (MSP command post) · Archie, Lt. Neil Brannock, Tess · three, formal.**
**Pursuit:** Archie (no lawyer) wants to convince them it's the trumpet.

**Required facts:** this reuses the controls footage — what's new is the thermos washed, the monkshood, the toot dispute, and Archie's status.
**Value:** witness → person of interest.
**Exit:** Archie consents to a search of the garden ("search the trunk if you can open it"); Brannock: stay away from his witnesses. Archie: he lives here.

### Ch 17 — Malvolio
**Mon Oct 27, ~6 p.m. · Hollis's house on Pudding Lane: front room of costumes on dress forms, kitchen · Archie, Hollis, a cat named Malvolio · two, warm.**
**Pursuit:** Archie came to borrow costumes for the show (and because he can't face home as a suspect). He wants to be told he's not a fool.

**Development:** supper (fish chowder; her bark of a laugh) → she makes him try on costumes; he tries on too many → he tells her about the lieutenant; she tells him not to let a man in a Dunkin' fleece make him small → she remembers Gus's last show in 1988 (she was twenty-nine, in the balcony); a detail Archie never knew → photos on the piano: Archie asks about a boy on a dock; "Danny. My son. He'd be thirty-nine." She moves on — "the sea's greedy here" — and he doesn't press → outside, her fall border cut down to the ground and the bed turned over ("putting the garden to bed") — one line, in passing, as he leaves with an armful of costumes.

**Exit:** Archie goes home lighter — and finds June waiting up with her laptop.

### Ch 18 — Harborview Antiques
**Mon Oct 27, ~10 p.m. · Fairweather House, June's room (the one with no secret doors) · Archie, June · two.**
**Pursuit:** June has found something and wants to be in the case, on her own terms.
**Counterpressure:** Archie wants her kept out (protective); he also can't resist.

### Ch 19 — Town Business
**Tue Oct 28, ~7 a.m. · The Galley, breakfast crowd · Archie, Warren, Bev, Hollis, liars' table, Shannon, Chief Pinkham · full room.**
**Pursuit:** Archie confronts Warren with the eBay listings and his theory — as a performance.
**Counterpressure:** Warren wants his dignity and his breakfast; he blusters, then can't.

### Ch 20 — Outside. Now.

### Ch 21 — Spectral Kev
**Tue Oct 28, ~10 a.m. · Kev's Honda Element, crammed with ghost-hunting gear, parked at the Merrow Light lookout · Archie, Kev · two.**
**Pursuit:** Archie wants Kev's secret.
