## Deviations from briefs, with reasons
- **Ch 17:** the brief says "her bark of a laugh." Hollis barks once, at the Puss-in-Boots boot. Archie never states his want to "be told he's not a fool"; instead Hollis tells him plainly ("You're not one. You're a show-off… it isn't fatal"). Archie isn't avoiding home because of the garden search but because of a news van. I kept the garden search out of this chapter so the reader never sees the troopers in Archie's garden next to Hollis's cut-down border. The border is one clause under the porch light, with no plant named.
- **Ch 20:** for the exit I wrote my own version rather than the brief's wording: "That man wears his own face on his sweatshirt. He's never kept a secret from an audience in his life." / "He's my witness, Archie." The chapter ends on Archie heading for Kev.
- **Ch 21:** in the exit I rephrased Archie's line as "You're getting a thank-you, Kev. The arrest is for somebody else." Kev's "money" question gets a comic answer ("The platform! The mattress!"). The faith crisis is built partly on Kev already having seen his own footage, and that reveal comes last, as briefed.
- **Ch 22:** the brief says Brannock "holds Archie for a formal recorded statement." It is voluntary on page ("free to leave… I'd rather you didn't"), and Trooper Lavoie takes it, to match Ch 23. Ivy's quitting argument (overheard by Archie in Ch 6) is not in Brannock's list, because I couldn't confirm Archie ever told the police. Brannock's list is the wipe, the hands lie, the remote prints, the scarf and the inheritance.
