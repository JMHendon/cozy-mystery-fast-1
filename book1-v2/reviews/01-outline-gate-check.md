# Outline-review questions (cozy-mystery.md, "Commissioning a book")

**Calendar first.** The death is in Ch 1. The days before the séance (arrival, challenge, Hollis's "beat her") are folded into after-death encounters where each changes something now: the toot/tea footage makes Archie a suspect (Ch 5); "Beat her" is his regret and Hollis's absolution (Ch 4); the readings are Shannon's grief (Ch 14). Loss recorded: the reader never sees Archie and Lorelei spar in daylight before the séance; their rivalry is felt in the dark (Ch 1) and in the Challenge terms. Accepted: gate 1 outranks it.

**Compression costs.** Five days for the investigation compresses forensics; three turnarounds are labelled conventions in the plan and are never stated as medical fact on the page. Gain: a hard clock (Halloween special, Halloween show) and the anniversary night as climax.

**Character contract.** Checked the main encounters against the cards:
- Ivy (guarded): confides only under the warrant (Ch 23, "It's my fault," which she means as fraud) and in a formal statement with a lawyer (Ch 31, reported). Not chummy with Archie at any point.
- Tess (rule-breaking "rarely, deliberately, and she'll pay"): bends once (Ch 19, a result he'll hear at nine anyway) and breaks once (Ch 31). **Fix applied:** Ch 41 now carries the cost (Brannock: "We'll talk about the porch at Sea View").
- Vivienne keeps "Sparrow" to herself throughout; the binder supplies it (Ch 41). Consistent with "He said when he's gone."
- Lenny's one sincere line placed Ch 40.
- Brannock holds honest wrong answers on real evidence (tea → Archie; then Ivy via the faked-toot branch). He is never stupid.

**Encounters, not interviews.** Interview-shaped chapters (5, 20, 28) each have a group engine and a change now: Ch 5 is the first time the reader sees the toot and tea (new evidence, not a retelling); Ch 20 turns on Archie's choice to protect Shannon; Ch 28 is a magician reading tape against a detective. Ch 39's confession is structured as Archie telling *her*, so the reader isn't walked back through events; Hollis corrects and confirms.

**Four clocks.** Kept separate in mystery-plan.md; Archie's tea is why Phase 1 survives the tox and dies only at the lab.

**A life worth joining.** Pleasures not tied to clue delivery: the hearse séance (12), poker at Bev's (17), midnight eggs (18), drywall at Pound Hill (19), hauling a trap (25), the network call (26), eleven pies (29), the Empty Chair with June (35), breakfast at the Galley (43), the trunk (44).

**Companionship.** Protected exchanges: Archie/Tess sparring in 2, 4, 12, 19, 22, 31, 36, 38, 43; Archie/June debate in 3, 10, 18, 26, 32; Archie/Vivienne in 3, 24, 40; Archie/Bev throughout.

**Experience and inference.** After each act, what a first-time reader would suspect: Act 1, Ivy (the wipe) or Warren (Archie's theory) or Archie himself. Act 2A, Ivy (police) or Kev; Hollis has appeared four times in ordinary warmth with nothing pointing at her. Act 2B, the forty seconds names four people near the table; a sharp reader may now look at Hollis, which is fair; the motive is withheld until the ledger (Ch 33). **Risk noted:** Ch 28 shows two Hollis facts on one tape (candles at 7:46, the slide at 7:51). Mitigation: the tape is also Ivy's rod, Shannon's fingertip, Kev's photo, Warren leaving and Vivienne's shawl; the camera holds the table, not Hollis; the room's argument is about Archie's toot and Ivy.
