# A cold read of Chapters 1–4

## 1. Where I was tempted to stop

I never seriously wanted to quit. Chapter 1 earns its hook, and "The fun went out of it all at once, like a breaker tripping" is the best sentence in the four chapters. These are the places I skimmed.

- **Chapter 1, the flashback.** The block starting "Half an hour ago, with the house lights up and two cameras on him, he had searched everything Lorelei had brought…" freezes a scene I was enjoying so the book can plant the tea and the trumpet. The planting is necessary. I could still feel it being done.
- **Chapter 3, from "Dad. Are you going to make this a show?" through the rules deal.** This is where I'd have put the book down.
  - "The tea was at twenty to eight. She was fine for forty minutes…" retells Chapter 1 to Lenny, who isn't the reader.
  - The teenager managing her father is the most familiar beat in the stretch: "You're doing the hands."
  - The outcome is obvious. The "no cameras / no cape / no hearse" list is built to be broken.
  - The scene was saved by "I was going to say *puzzle*" and by the trunk scene after it.
- **Chapter 4, after page one.** The liars' table, the bell over the door, "Ayuh" and the pie "As a protest" are pleasant, but I've been to this diner.
- **The pattern.** Chapters 2, 3 and 4 each put Archie in front of a new listener (Calloway, June, the Galley) and have him recite the same séance facts. By the third recital I wanted new information.

## 2. Where I expected something that didn't come

- **The footage.** The cameras "went dark at once" at 8:14. In Chapter 3 Archie says "somebody filmed it." In Chapter 4 "Lieutenant Brannock watched the footage at two this morning." Nobody says what's on it.
- **Ivy.** She tells a detective, "She held my hand. The whole time." We know that's false. I expected Calloway to pounce, and she just writes it down. Ivy has about six lines in four chapters.
- **Warren's whisper.** Archie says Lorelei whispered to Warren and Warren "went the color of skim milk." That is not on the page in Chapter 1. The book's main suspect is built on something I never saw.
- **The "notebook of failures."** It's mentioned once in Chapter 3 and never explained.
- **The trunk.** It has "Seven brass letter-dials." Archie says "Seven letters," then asks "Who's Sparrow?" Sparrow is seven letters, so I was a chapter ahead of a man who solves puzzles for a living. Either he knows and is waiting, or the book let him miss it. I couldn't tell which, and that doubt cost him credibility.

## 3. Funniest and most magnetic

- **Funniest line:** Archie's: "*Amos, it's your mother. Put a sweater on.*"
- **Most magnetic: Lorelei Crane**, in about fifteen lines.
  - "Brother Amos is with us. He senses a skeptic."
  - Her laugh, "low and surprised and real."
  - "Not… Not the spirits."
  - I wanted a whole book of her against Archie, so killing her costs the novel its best foil.
- **Close behind: Calloway.** "Do you," with no question mark. She's the only person who wins an exchange with him: "you look like you'd count your fingers afterward." / "I would."
- **Also close:**
  - Bev: "You did. As a protest." and "You were a lot more pleasant at nine." / "I was nine."
  - Vivienne: "It is a *centerpiece*."
  - Margaret Okafor, who is funny in six lines: "If this is not about a wedding cake, I'm going to have you killed."
  - June: "That's three words."
- **Weakest:** Pinkham, Teddy and Kev are types. "Only emotionally" is a groaner, delivered while a woman is dying.

## 4. Be him, or spend time with him?

I'd spend time with him, not be him.

- **What I like:** he delights in other people's craft. "Lorelei, that's the best float I've ever seen." He hopes someone wins his challenge, and he'd buy his opponent a lobster dinner.
- **Best moment:** the sticker. He shows off, gets it wrong, and sees it hurt. It's the only time he's wrong in a way that costs him anything.
- **Why I wouldn't be him:** he's right about every trick and every hand, and the prose keeps telling us he's brilliant. Even his lapse at the Galley ("Hear me out, though") is played as charm. I want him to lose something. "He could make forty thousand people believe a woman had vanished" is also told, not shown.

## 5. What I think happened, and who

**Theory.** The narrow end of the trumpet was dosed after Archie's clean test at 7:36. "Pins… all my face" matches a mouthpiece dose, and Archie drank the same tea and is fine. The poison doubles as a frame. Archie poured the public tea, blew into the trumpet, is the Borgia-poison guy, and there's monkshood in his kitchen.

Two other things stand out:

- Shannon was recruited in April ("A girl called me in April. Free reading, because of Tommy"), five months before Archie bought the building. So the operation predates his challenge and was aimed at Merrow. Someone local fed the team "Viv," "Vivi" and "Sparrow."
- The voice that "smeared" mid-sentence ("The answer's in the—") may not have been showmanship. It may have been the poison landing, or someone stopping her. Her last words, "Not the spirits," suggest she knew a person did it.

**Ranking:**

1. **Ivy.**
   - She lied about the hand.
   - She wiped the trumpet with her scarf and pocketed it.
   - She wouldn't take off the coat.
   - She said "She doesn't drink anything I don't pour" and then let her mother drink.
   - Against her: the collapse ("Okay," then folding "as if a string had been cut") and no visible motive.
   - She's the one the book points at hardest, so she may be covering for someone, or for a gimmick. I think she's the person most likely to know.
2. **Warren Gilley.** He's the dread-stricken chair of the board, with a sale clause hanging over Archie. But he reaches me only through Archie, and the chapter ends "Then I'd better be right." That's how books say he may not be.
3. **Hollis Tibbetts.** She alone ran the board in the booth and knows the building. Nobody has looked at her, and in this genre unexamined competence is a flag. The evidence is thin and the instinct is strong.
4. **Teddy Marchand.** He's the producer, with a Friday airdate and "We had a *plan*." He was also the man at the monitor saying "Static, static, love it, keep going."
5. **Vivienne.** She isn't a killer, but she's hiding something: "Don't you dare. Not yet." and "Go to bed, Archibald."

I don't suspect Kev, Shannon, Lenny, Bev or Pinkham. Bev's washed thermos reads as a red herring staged to make Archie look guilty.

**Side guess:** SPARROW opens the trunk, and Sparrow is a person, maybe someone at Sea View, the memory-care home where Calloway's sticker came from.

## 6. Would I read Chapter 5?

Yes, right now. I'd go on for Sparrow, the trunk and Calloway's sticker, more than for the murder mechanics. What I want next:

- The 9 a.m. interview with Brannock, with Margaret there, in which Archie gets out-argued for once.
- The footage, even if it only deepens the mystery.
- Ivy interviewed, with her lie put to her.
- Calloway and her mother.
- A scene where Archie is wrong about a trick.
- Someone other than Archie explaining something.

## 7. What I reread, couldn't picture, or didn't believe

- **The seating.** Lorelei is "across," Shannon is at her right, Ivy at her left, Warren is "two seats to Archie's right, past Vivienne," and Kev is on Archie's left. I tried to draw it and couldn't tell whose hand Ivy's other hand was in. That matters because the central clue is "Ivy let go." One sentence would fix it.
- **The cameras.** They die at 8:14, then become the alibi in Chapter 3.
- **The monkshood.** Vivienne cut it "this afternoon," and it turns out to be the same poison, in the suspect's kitchen. The line "If I were framing me, this is exactly what I'd do" acknowledges the coincidence, but I only half-bought it.
- **Archie's judgment at the Galley.** He says "Warren Gilley. Twenty minutes before the lights went out…" in a room of Warren's relatives, hours after promising June. It reads as the plot needing him to be reckless.
- **The scarves.** "Gus's eight-foot emerald scarf" turns up in Chapter 2 right beside the gray silk scarf that is the key clue. For a second I thought they were the same one.
