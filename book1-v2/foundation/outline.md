# Outline

**Shape:** 44 chapters, each one scene under 2,000 words (targets 1,600–1,950; book target ~79,000 words). Five days, Monday October 27 (the séance) to Friday October 31 (Halloween night, the climax), then a three-chapter restoration ending on Saturday November 15, the day the opera house sale closes.

**The structural answer to the retry:** the book opens *inside* the séance, in the dark, with Archie winning. Everything the old plan staged before the death (the challenge, the controls, the toot, the tea, Archie's history with Lorelei, Shannon's readings, Hollis talking him out of cancelling) is delivered around the death or afterwards, each where it changes something now: the toot and the tea arrive as evidence against Archie in Ch 5, "Beat her" arrives as Archie's regret in Ch 4, Shannon's readings arrive as her grief in Ch 14.

## Acts

### Act 1 — Beginning Hook (Ch 1–11; 0–25%) · "The Dark"
- **Inciting incident:** Ch 1–2. Lorelei collapses through her own trumpet; dies at 9:40.
- **Turning point (progressive complications):** Ch 10–11. Warren moves to void the sale and Brannock, in front of the whole town, names Archie as the man he needs to talk to about monkshood. Archie's theater and his name are both at risk.
- **Crisis:** Ch 11. Stand back (lawyer's advice, June's "regular year," the sale) or investigate in public, under suspicion.
- **Climax:** Ch 11. In front of the town, Archie tells Brannock and Tess he'll find who did it, and does it with June at his side as his "counsel."
- **Resolution:** Ch 12. Tess comes to the carriage house for a statement and lets him show her how the trick was done; the partnership exists, grudgingly.
- **Value:** justice: invisible crime → crime suspected, wrong man suspected. Internal: Archie performs at everyone → June at the table as counsel.

### Act 2A — Middle Build I (Ch 12–22; 25–50%) · "Everybody Knows the Trick"
- **Inciting incident:** Ch 12. In the back of the hearse Tess recites "lips to the small end": the whole town learned the method in Mrs. Tibbetts's class.
- **Turning point:** Ch 19. The OCME's targeted screen is positive: it's murder, and Brannock is coming for Archie at nine.
- **Crisis:** Ch 21. Kev's hidden footage would clear Archie and sink Ivy, whom Archie believes innocent, and break Kev's trust; keep it back or hand it over.
- **Climax:** Ch 22. He brings Kev and the footage to Tess.
- **Resolution (midpoint):** Ch 22. The lab finds aconitine inside the trumpet's small end. Archie's understanding reverses: not *who hated her* but *who knew she would put her mouth there*. The police turn from Archie to Ivy.
- **Value:** justice: wrong suspect (Archie) → a different wrong suspect (Ivy). Internal: Archie solo → Archie with Tess, Kev, June.

### Act 2B — Middle Build II (Ch 23–33; 50–75%) · "The Forty Seconds"
- **Inciting incident:** Ch 23. The warrant at the inn; Ivy's scarf seized; "It's my fault."
- **Turning point:** Ch 30. Ivy is charged with murder, partly on evidence Archie supplied.
- **Crisis:** Ch 30–31. Go home and do his Halloween show, as Brannock says, or keep going with nothing but a dead woman's shorthand.
- **Climax:** Ch 31–32. He accepts what Tess breaks a rule to give him, and opens the hatbox, where June reveals she can read the code.
- **Resolution:** Ch 33. The ledger: *P.L., Merrow.* The man in Maine was from here. Who is H. Langley?
- **Value:** justice: injustice deepening (an innocent woman charged). Internal: June let backstage.

### Act 3 — Ending Payoff (Ch 34–44; 75–100%) · "Shag Ledge"
- **Inciting incident:** Ch 34. "Langley? That's Hollis." The click.
- **Turning point:** Ch 35. Mid-show, the booth is empty; an envelope for Sergeant Calloway on the dimmer board.
- **Crisis:** Ch 36. Hollis has taken the harbor skiff into the fog toward Shag Ledge; wait for the Marine Patrol, or go after her in a small boat he gets sick in.
- **Climax:** Ch 37–38. He goes, and goes into the water.
- **Resolution:** Ch 39–44. Confession confirms; Ivy freed; Hollis visited; the town resets; the trunk opens.
- **Value:** justice achieved at a cost. Internal: backstage, with June and Vivienne at the trunk.

### Ch 1 — The Trumpet
*Mon Oct 27 · 8:00–8:35 p.m. · Opera house stage, séance table in the dark · Target 1,900*
**Cast:** Archie; Vivienne (his left hand); Kev (his right); Lorelei; Ivy; Warren; Shannon; Teddy (voice from the wing); Hollis (voice from the booth; the lights); Pinkham (at the back). **Scale:** full room.
- **Pursuit:** beat Lorelei Crane on camera in his grandfather's theater, under the terms of the Fairweather Challenge: name the method of every "phenomenon" as it happens. And, secretly, find out whether anything in here is real.
- **Counterpressure:** Lorelei is superb and wants the special and the room; Vivienne wants to hear her father's name said in this room and grips Archie's hand when he whispers; Kev, a believer, keeps whispering "Did you feel that?"; Shannon wants her husband.
- **Development:** Archie calls each method in a whisper to Vivienne (raps = toe, hand release, bell on a rod, damp glove, "static" = a remote), and is right, and is enjoying himself out loud → "Tommy" speaks through the trumpet to Shannon, and Archie's fun curdles: he wants to stop it and can't without breaking the circle and Shannon's moment → "Gus" speaks: *Viv… Sparrow… the trunk* — and Archie, who wants one of them to be real, is hooked; Vivienne's hand clamps → the voice thickens and slurs; he thinks it's craft → "My mouth— I can't feel my—"; the trumpet falls.
- **5C:** II the lights go out and the contest starts / TP the voice slurs and Lorelei says she can't feel her mouth / Crisis: hold the circle (the rule of the room, the show, his mother's moment with "Gus") or break it / Climax: he breaks it: "Lights! Hollis — lights!" and goes to her / Resolution: in the light he sees Ivy wipe the trumpet's small end with her silk scarf and push it into her coat; he knows a numb mouth from his old Borgia research and tells Pinkham to tell the paramedics *aconite*; Pinkham ropes off the table.
- **Value:** play/contest (+) → life in danger, a crime only Archie can name (−). Justice: no position → a poisoning suspected.
- **Facts:** circle order from Lorelei's left: Ivy, Warren, Vivienne, Archie (opposite her, back to the house), Kev, Shannon (her right). Archie holds Vivienne's and Kev's hands. One open window at stage left (harbor lights). Red spirit lamp. Phenomena and times per mystery-plan §Séance. Earlier on camera (7:30–7:41) Archie inspected the props, blew a raspberry through the trumpet ("no spirits in here"), was refused a search and the seat beside her, and poured her chamomile from Bev's thermos ("the tea truce"); she drank half. These may be recalled in a clause or two; they are evidence later. Lorelei calls people "honey." Archie had searched the table: he knows the trumpet is aluminum with luminous bands. "Sparrow" means nothing to him. Hollis is a voice and a light board only.
- **Exit:** Lorelei, gray, lips working, looks at Archie: one performer to another (*optional:* "Not the spirits, honey."). The paramedics are coming up the stairs. Archie: "Nobody touches that trumpet."
- **Variation:** n/a (first chapter).

### Ch 2 — Nine-Forty
*Mon · 9:05–9:50 p.m. · Family room, emergency department, the hospital in Rockport · Target 1,850*
**Cast:** Archie, Vivienne, Ivy, Teddy, Dr. Anil Rao, Tess (arrives ~9:20). **Scale:** small group.
- **Pursuit:** make sure somebody orders a targeted aconitine screen before the chance is gone, because nobody tests for it unless asked; and, under that, will Lorelei live?
- **Counterpressure:** Dr. Rao is fighting for her life and has no time for a celebrity; Ivy, next of kin, wants this stranger who came to destroy her mother out of the room; Teddy, on two phones, wants the narrative ("tragic, natural, the spirits took her" is a better promo than "poison") and the footage under his control; Tess, sent because Merrow is her town and she was ten minutes away at Sea View, wants facts and thinks Archie is making a death about himself.
- **Development:** Archie works Rao in the corridor: fame bounces off; precise symptoms land ("numbness and tingling of the lips, then the voice, then the heart; you'll see a ventricular arrhythmia that doesn't respond") → Rao: "Who are you?" and goes back in → Tess arrives; first exchange is a collision (she's met performers; he flirts; she doesn't) → Ivy, to Tess: "She held my hand. The whole time." Archie knows that is a lie and that Ivy wiped the mouthpiece → Rao comes out: dead at 9:40.
- **5C:** II Archie can't get the doctor to hear him / TP Lorelei dies; Ivy has just lied to a police officer / Crisis: say what he saw in front of a daughter whose mother died four minutes ago (cruel, maybe wrong) or keep it and let the evidence walk out in her coat / Climax: he tells Tess, quietly and exactly, the wipe and the pocket, and the word *aconitine* / Resolution: Tess makes no promise, then turns to Rao: hold the antemortem blood and flag it for the medical examiner, targeted screen, aconitine. She tells Archie to go home. He goes, having won the only thing he came for.
- **Value:** life → death. Justice: invisible → possible (screen requested; the wipe on record).
- **Facts:** pronounced dead 9:40 p.m.; refractory arrhythmia. Archie drank his own cup of the tea; Rao checks his lips (no symptoms; Archie has a line). Tess has not yet seen any footage. Tess's father is at Sea View; she doesn't explain. Archie calls her "Sergeant" from the first minute. Teddy mentions the special airs Friday, Halloween night. - **Exit:** Tess: "Go home, Mr. Fairweather." Archie, plain for once: "I liked her." Then the car park, the hearse, his mother.
