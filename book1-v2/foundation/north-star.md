# North star

## Premise

The world's most famous magician bought a crumbling opera house in a Maine harbor town so his teenage daughter could have one real year. On the Monday of Halloween week he sits in the séance circle of America's favorite TV medium, on her stage, in his grandfather's theater, to beat her on camera, and he is winning, method by method, in the dark, until the spirit trumpet falls and Lorelei Crane says she can't feel her mouth. Every hand in the circle was held. The only man in the room who knows what a numb mouth means is the man who blew a raspberry through her trumpet half an hour earlier and poured her a cup of tea on camera. He has five days, a skeptical State Police sergeant, a hearse, and the best lawyer in New England on speed dial, and he is having the time of his life.

## Reader promise

The crime is serious; the world is gentle; the company is wonderful. You will spend a Halloween week in Merrow, Maine, with a man who is the funniest and most delighted person in every room, who sees how impossible things are done because he does them for a living, and who is falling, very slowly and against her will, into a partnership with the one woman he can't charm. You will see every clue. You will laugh on most pages. The ending will make you cry once and grin twice, and the last page will make you need Book 2.

## Controlling idea

**External (crime):** Justice is restored when someone who knows how the trick is done refuses to look away from the people being fooled, including the people he loves.

**Internal (worldview):** Love deepens when we let the people we love backstage; wonder shared is better than wonder performed.

## Series engine

- **The partnership.** Archie and Tess: wonder vs procedure, performance vs truth, delight vs rigor, slow burn. Each book she trusts him one notch further; each book he learns one more thing he can't charm. No kiss in Book 1.
- **The place.** Merrow and its opera house, which Archie now owns and programs; every book can bring a show, a festival or a visitor to its stage.
- **The family.** Archie as the kid, June/Vivienne/Lenny as the grown-ups; June's magic, Vivienne's courtship with Ozzie.
- **The long mystery.** Gus Fairweather vanished from the harbor on Halloween 1988. Book 1 ends with proof he was alive in 1996 and a chief of police who says "Gus Fairweather still owes me a boat." Tess still has a three-word letter from Archie that he doesn't remember writing.
- **The case type.** Impossible crimes in plain sight, solved by a magician's eye for method and misdirection, fairly clued.
