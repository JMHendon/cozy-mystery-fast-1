# Final Proofread

There are six changes in total. Each one changes a word or a phrase. No events changed, no prose was restyled, and no protected line was touched.

## Seams checked, no change needed

- **Shannon at 7:45.** Ch 7 is consistent: she touches the horn when Archie reaches the back, just before "a quarter hour to go." So are Ch 26 (time-coded at 7:45), Ch 28 ("questions about seven forty-five"), Ch 33 (the gloss is gone, with nothing left dangling) and Ch 41 ("on that horn").
- **Deb's card (Ch 5) against Ch 32 and Ch 35.** The card order matches the ledger: Coombs, Thibodeau, Calloway, Pelletier, Pinkham, Langley, then June three times. Deb passes over H. Langley by mentioning the senior-English unit, so June's "Somebody named H. Langley" and "The last name before mine. I've signed under it three times" both hold. Ch 35's "*H. Langley, December 2003*" matches too.
- **Ch 41/42 order.** Ch 41 is Keys: Pen Bay, room 214, from 10:30. It ends with "The Galley… She gave me some things to deliver." Ch 42 is Daylight: the Galley at noon, with "I saw Hollis this morning," Ivy "two hours out" of jail, and the godmother message delivered. The headings and the time cues are correct.
- **Vivienne's cloak line (Ch 40 against Ch 44).** Ch 40 has "Ozzie says you did this. On the boat. Before you went in." / "I don't remember doing it." with no explanation. Ch 39 has "no time to wonder who had folded it." Ch 44 then has him remember the fold, and "Folded." lands there for the first time.
- **"Holy Houdini" removal.** There are no uses left in the manuscript and nothing dangles from the cut in Ch 22. The only "Houdini" left is the séance-unit reading list in Ch 3.
- **Kayla.** She is named everywhere ("a young officer named Kayla," Ch 3), and "Dale's girl" appears nowhere. ("Dale's cousin's girl" in Ch 5 is M. Pinkham on the library card, as in the ledger.)
- **Mechanical checks:** no doubled words ("whatever you do do," Ch 18, is intentional), no unbalanced quotes or italics, no comma splices between dialogue and action beats, no missing terminal punctuation, and no misspelled words or variant spellings of names or places.

## Noted, deliberately not changed

These fall outside "minimal fix", or they read as character voice rather than error.

1. **Circle handedness.** Ch 7's seating order (Ivy at Lorelei's left, then Warren, Vivienne, Archie…) puts Vivienne on Archie's right if you work out the geometry, but Ch 8 has "his mother's… fingers… on his left." No two chapters state conflicting sides outright, and changing it would ripple into the trumpet's "left" in Ch 8, 9 and 21.
2. **Ch 22:** Brannock says Kev's footage "spent seventy-two hours" on his laptop. It's closer to sixty-two, but it reads as the lieutenant rounding.
3. **Ch 29:** Lenny is "in all three of his sweaters." Ch 1 gives him three plus a fourth around his shoulders. It's a comic detail, not a plot fact.
4. **Ch 30:** the italic memory of Kev's Hiatus speech paraphrases rather than quotes Ch 21. It's framed as Archie's recollection.
