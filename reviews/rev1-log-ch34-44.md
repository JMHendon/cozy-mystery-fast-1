## Sweep figures (section C)

- **Manner clauses** ("the way X…", "as if/as though", "like a man who…", "of a man who"): about 40 in the draft, about 11 now. That is roughly one per 1,650 words, never two in a paragraph, and at most one per scene. The survivors carry information. Examples: Carol checking that WARREN is "still there"; Hollis looking at him "the way she might have looked at a student who had finally… read the book"; "the way stage lights hold when nobody is touching the board"; Vivienne's protected "exactly the way my father used to fold it"; Hollis lifting the cone "the way anybody picks up a funnel" (this one is the method itself). Every "the way it does/did" vehicle is gone, and so are all five "the way you'd [steady/shade/grip/hand] a…" clauses.
- **"X stopped." one-liners**: 35 ("The fork stopped in the ground.") and 40 ("He stopped crawling."). No more than one per chapter.
- **"for a long moment"**: none left in 34–44. "for a long time" is used once (41, Hollis before "Ask me in a year").
- **"something went across/over her face" / "face did something"**: all cut. That includes 38 (Hollis, twice), 41 (Warren), 43 (Archie's face), 44 (Vivienne) and 40 ("His voice did something").
- **"very X and very Y"**: all four pairs cut. Those were 36 ("very white and very set"), 37 and 39 ("very small and very clear"), and 44 ("very quiet and very steady"). Single "very" is down to about 9 across the range.
- **Body-before-mind**: kept once, for the cloak fold in 38 (the Act III allowance). Cut "He didn't decide anything. His hands were already doing it" (38), "without quite deciding to" (34, Tess), "He'd driven to the wharf without deciding to" (43), "found, to his complete surprise" (36), "his body knew how to do that without him… heard himself" (36), "He felt the grin start somewhere around his ribs" (44) and "found that he was thinking" (38).
- **Capped images**: "whole room in a second and a half" cut (37). "pushed straight through it, the way Shannon had" cut (37). "struck match" kept once (44). "meant it so much that she looked faintly startled" cut (Ivy, now 42). "1994" punchline cut (36, "in living memory"). The "four sweaters" mentions are gone (36 → "a headset over his sweaters"; 44 → "one sweater fewer than usual"). "Merrow Light every six seconds" is cut from 44.
- **Banned tics**: "Tess's jaw was set" (39), "let out a breath" (35) and "chuckled" (34) are gone.
- **Chapter endings**: only Ch 43 closes on a harbor image (Merrow Light and the bell buoy, kept on purpose). Ch 39 used to end on the bell buoy "for nobody"; it now ends on Ozzie's truck pulling out. The other endings are: a text (34); action (35, 37, 38, 39); dialogue (36, 40, 41, 44); and "Correct answer" with the table going out (42).
- **American English, profanity**: Bev's "Oh, for God's sake" (36) became "Oh, for crying out *loud*". The Britishism check came up clean.
