# Register Standard — light-energy

## The four constitutive properties

1. **The protagonist's fundamental weather is delight.** In ordinary circumstances, interest tends to become participation: she asks, tries, joins or recruits instead of repeatedly considering whether to do so. Work may be part of the pleasure. So may looking magnificent, playing to win, trying a toy or pursuing a wonderful meal: her first interest need not be practicality or self-improvement. Her curiosity and appetite survive pressure; they are not a requirement to enjoy suffering or entertain people in an acute emergency. Genuine shock or grief can interrupt the ordinary buoyancy. Quiet enjoyment and listening are not energy failures.
2. **Comedy belongs to the continuing life of the book.** Do not segregate it into designated relief scenes, but do not require a joke inside every grave exchange. A character's living voice can survive a passage without a punchline.
3. **Emotional payoffs need contrast, not a mandatory tonal sandwich.** Humour, work, pleasure and silence can carry the characters onward. The audience may retain a hurt the protagonist has not fully understood; sustained stillness belongs where the events earn it.
4. **The world is fundamentally fixable.** Not nihilist, not tragic; even terrible things happen inside a narrative trust that this world makes sense.

*(What light-energy is NOT: heavy-energy communal healing, classical cleverness-over-warmth, literary mood, procedural-with-comic-relief.)*

## The register tests (qualitative, not quotas)

- **Where does gravity come from?** Distinguish actual danger, grief or accumulated damage from a writer making the protagonist solemn to prove she cares. Serious stakes do not require continuous serious delivery.
- **Delight is not compliance.** Enjoyable interviewing can involve evasion, resistance and changing tactics. A guarded person need not become companionable to supply warmth or energy. Preserve each person's default conduct and the relationship's actual stage; a private room or a laugh does not earn a private confession.
- **Consequence is not confession.** Report what happens to another person, what the protagonist believes happened, and what she changes. Do not require these to coincide. A refused invitation can still stand after she recovers her spirits.
- **No prescribed recovery sequence.** Deflection, a partial apology, irritation, a practical kindness, silence or renewed pleasure are possible. Do not require a joke after an honest line or a moral lesson after a failed joke.
- **POV-wit, not constant narrator display.** The narration can be companionable and have opinions; it need not explain or decorate each human reaction.
- **Fumbles may simply puncture.** Do not turn every domestic failure into earned access, information or proof of virtue. For this series, arthritis is never part of the comedy.
- **Plain speech and uneven responses give wit contrast.** Listen for literal answers, missed jokes, interruption and independent tasks. Another person can sustain a delightful exchange without matching the lead's polish, granting access or losing their own purpose. Enjoyment is not intimacy. Do not allocate a numerical share of flat exchanges or use asymmetry to remove the lead's distinctive charm.

## The energy tests

- **What actually stopped her?** For a contemplative pause before an ordinary action, identify the cause. Preserve meaningful uncertainty, restraint, grief, fatigue and absorption; remove an unnecessary private debate rather than decorating it with a smile. A small change that restores forward motion is a successful revision; more changed text is not inherently better.
- **What does she want to do, not just discover?** Let some pleasures shape destinations, company, choices and time. A game need not yield evidence; enjoyable work need not be interrupted by self-analysis. Choose the occasion that belongs to this book rather than inserting a tour of the card's eccentricities. For the private pleasure to register, she need not have an audience.
- **Does abundance create pleasure?** Penn ignores rank and shares money, access, enthusiasm and connections. She often solves the practical problem herself; a successful generous invitation needs no hidden moral bill. Occasionally she offers the wrong solution or assumes it is wanted. Preserve the other person's choice without routinely making generosity inconvenient or using refusal to teach her a lesson.
- **Let an encounter breathe.** Protect conversational pleasure as well as active appetite. An extravagant invitation, an excuse to stay or a shared absurdity can arise from the present want; it is not a detachable punchline. Give the other person's response room to affect the exchange. Not every exchange needs a capper. Ask whether the protagonist enjoys this particular person's company and whether we want to hear them continue once the information is delivered; exchange counts and joke counts do not measure that pleasure.
- **Place pleasure deliberately.** Is this the right place and amount of space for this pleasure? That is not the question “does it advance the investigation?” A central movement organises a chapter without explaining every paragraph. Preserve a wanted game or exchange that builds company, contrast or attachment; relocate one when its present placement repeatedly restarts the chapter. Do not give it a clue simply to justify keeping it.
- **Give the reader footing.** Information may travel with wit, action or warmth, but useful plain orientation is not a lecture. Flag sustained explanation that replaces a playable encounter, not every paragraph without a joke.
- **Play consequential changes.** Show the important refusal, choice or lost access. Minor losses and routine actions can be compressed.
- **Check premature moral closure.** If each social overreach ends in a perceptive rebuke and humble insight, the character is reforming several times a chapter. Let the reader understand an asymmetry before the protagonist does; keep real consequences and delayed learning distinct.

## The climax + coda shape

Chase/confrontation (jeopardy scaled to the body; humour-as-armour may still flicker) → the cracking moment (the case becomes demonstrable; procedure follows) → room for an earned private exchange → restoration (the community resets, altered; warmth with specificity, no speeches). Proportions and placement are book decisions. A series continuation may sit inside the warm close, but do not add an ominous final hint merely because the long arc exists; an earlier, ordinary-seeming seed may be enough.

## The B-story pattern

A book-length secondary strand gives someone a concern beyond the investigation, develops through encounters and choices, and reaches a meaningful change. It may echo the main story without explaining the theme or cresting at exactly the same moment. Review a book that has no life beyond its case at outline stage; do not manufacture a late subplot to pass a generation gate.

A series-long investigation is not the same obligation. An early book may hold one unrecognised fact inside a worthwhile encounter and leave its significance unresolved. Do not force a revelation, ominous aside or personal connection merely to make the seed register as a subplot; track that seed and its eventual development in the series plan.

## Failure modes (name them in review)

Unearned gravity runs · premature moral closure · comedy that undercuts a beat the reader needed to keep · narrator-wit drift · the lecture-payoff (a character explains the theme) · slapstick · a protagonist whose voice disappears whenever the subject becomes serious. Preserve earned grief rather than forcing cheerfulness; continuous sombre delivery is not the only form of respect.

**Efficient but joyless:** active events, competent answers and clear transitions survive, while the invitations, playful resistance and familiar exchanges that made the company desirable disappear. Compare before and after; a clearer chapter may still have suffered this regression. Restore the relationship-specific exchange, not a scattering of new one-liners.

**Ambient wit** (2026-08-29, first human cold read): every mouth polished, every exchange ending on somebody's crafted final word — one exceptionally clever conversationalist wearing the whole cast. Wit is owned, not ambient. Distinguish what people want, notice, do and sound like rather than rationing who is allowed to be a forceful presence. A host can command an audience through conviction, taste, attention or action while Penn remains an active, delighted participant. Do not flatten a major character to protect the lead's brightness, or give everyone Penn's repartee to make them memorable. Competence remains local and consequential.
