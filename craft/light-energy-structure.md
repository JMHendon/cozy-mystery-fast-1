# Register Standard — light-energy

Light-energy is the register of every cozy mystery (gate 14): warm, funny and fast, with real stakes. A cozy is never as dark as a thriller or a police procedural. Books vary in how much of their light is comedy and how much is warmth, but none of them goes dark: no dread for its own sake, no lingering on suffering, no world revealed to be rotten. The lead-presence section applies to every book.

## The four constitutive properties

1. **The lead's fundamental weather is delight.** In ordinary circumstances, interest tends to become participation: the lead asks, tries, joins or recruits instead of repeatedly considering whether to. Work may be part of the pleasure. So may looking magnificent, playing to win, trying a toy, showing off or pursuing a wonderful meal; the lead's first interest need not be practicality or self-improvement. Curiosity and appetite survive pressure; they are not a requirement to enjoy suffering or entertain people in an acute emergency. Genuine shock or grief can interrupt the ordinary buoyancy. Quiet enjoyment and listening are not energy failures.
2. **Comedy belongs to the continuing life of the book.** Do not segregate it into designated relief scenes, but do not require a joke inside every grave exchange. A character's living voice can survive a passage without a punchline.
3. **Emotional payoffs need contrast, not a mandatory tonal sandwich.** Humour, work, pleasure and silence can carry the characters onward. The audience may retain a hurt the lead has not fully understood; sustained stillness belongs where the events earn it.
4. **The world is fundamentally fixable.** Not nihilist, not tragic; even terrible things happen inside a narrative trust that this world makes sense.

*(What light-energy is NOT: heavy-energy communal healing, classical cleverness-over-warmth, literary mood, procedural-with-comic-relief.)*

## The lead owns the room

Readers come for the lead. Every register test below protects the lead's presence; none of them licenses shrinking it.

- **The lead is the brightest, most distinctive voice in most scenes.** Count it in the prose: who has the most lines, the best lines and the lines a reader would quote. If the answer is regularly someone else (a deadpan sidekick, a formidable elder, the narrator), the lead has been displaced.
- **The lead's wit is spoken, not narrated.** Wit that lives in the narration ("built like a fire hydrant that had given up on the world") reads as the author being clever. The lead's wit belongs in the lead's mouth, said to people's faces, where it can land, fail or provoke.
- **Restraint rules have a target.** The tests below against ambient wit, cappers, epigrams and polished final words apply to the supporting cast and the narrator. They are never a ceiling on the lead's talk, theories, showmanship or delight. Where a book's lead card names a reference character, the lead card governs the lead's register.
- **Show the lead at strength before showing the cost.** In the opening chapters the reader must see why this person is magnetic, competent and fun before seeing them humbled. Across any run of chapters the lead also wins, is right, delights someone and gets the better of someone.
- **Supporting characters shine in their own currency.** Literalism, plain speech, a task they keep doing, conviction, taste, silence or a different kind of authority. They may win exchanges and command a room without matching the lead's polish, and without flattening the lead to make room.

## The register tests (qualitative, not quotas)

- **Where does gravity come from?** Distinguish actual danger, grief or accumulated damage from a writer making the lead solemn to prove they care. Serious stakes do not require continuous serious delivery.
- **Delight is not compliance.** Enjoyable interviewing can involve evasion, resistance and changing tactics. A guarded person need not become companionable to supply warmth or energy. Preserve each person's default conduct and the relationship's actual stage; a private room or a laugh does not earn a private confession.
- **Consequence is not confession.** Report what happens to another person, what the lead believes happened, and what the lead changes. Do not require these to coincide. A refused invitation can still stand after the lead recovers their spirits.
- **No prescribed recovery sequence.** Deflection, a partial apology, irritation, a practical kindness, silence or renewed pleasure are possible. Do not require a joke after an honest line or a moral lesson after a failed joke.
- **POV-wit, not constant narrator display.** The narration can be companionable and have opinions; it need not explain or decorate each human reaction.
- **Fumbles may simply puncture.** Do not turn every domestic failure into earned access, information or proof of virtue. Never make a disability, illness or a character's body the joke.
- **Plain speech and uneven responses give wit contrast.** Listen for literal answers, missed jokes, interruption and independent tasks. Another person can sustain a delightful exchange without matching the lead's polish, granting access or losing their own purpose. Enjoyment is not intimacy. Do not allocate a numerical share of flat exchanges, and never use asymmetry to remove the lead's distinctive charm.

## The energy tests

- **What actually stopped the lead?** For a contemplative pause before an ordinary action, identify the cause. Preserve meaningful uncertainty, restraint, grief, fatigue and absorption; remove an unnecessary private debate rather than decorating it with a smile. A small change that restores forward motion is a successful revision; more changed text is not inherently better.
- **What does the lead want to do, not just discover?** Let some pleasures shape destinations, company, choices and time. A game need not yield evidence; enjoyable work need not be interrupted by self-analysis. Choose the occasion that belongs to this book rather than inserting a tour of the card's eccentricities. A private pleasure registers without an audience.
- **Does abundance create pleasure?** A generous lead shares money, access, enthusiasm and connections, and often solves the practical problem themself; a successful generous invitation needs no hidden moral bill. Occasionally the lead offers the wrong solution or assumes it is wanted. Preserve the other person's choice without routinely making generosity inconvenient or using refusal to teach the lead a lesson.
- **Let an encounter breathe.** Protect conversational pleasure as well as active appetite. An extravagant invitation, an excuse to stay or a shared absurdity can arise from the present want; it is not a detachable punchline. Give the other person's response room to affect the exchange. Not every exchange needs a capper. Ask whether the lead enjoys this particular person's company and whether we want to hear them continue once the information is delivered; exchange counts and joke counts do not measure that pleasure.
- **Place pleasure deliberately.** Is this the right place and amount of space for this pleasure? That is not the question "does it advance the investigation?" A central movement organises a chapter without explaining every paragraph. Preserve a wanted game or exchange that builds company, contrast or attachment; relocate one when its present placement repeatedly restarts the chapter. Do not give it a clue simply to justify keeping it.
- **Give the reader footing.** Information may travel with wit, action or warmth, but useful plain orientation is not a lecture. Flag sustained explanation that replaces a playable encounter, not every paragraph without a joke.
- **Play consequential changes.** Show the important refusal, choice or lost access. Minor losses and routine actions can be compressed.
- **Check premature moral closure.** If each social overreach ends in a perceptive rebuke and humble insight, the lead is reforming several times a chapter. Let the reader understand an asymmetry before the lead does; keep real consequences and delayed learning distinct.

## The climax + coda shape

Chase or confrontation (jeopardy scaled to the body; humour-as-armour may still flicker) → the cracking moment (the case becomes demonstrable; procedure follows) → room for an earned private exchange → restoration (the community resets, altered; warmth with specificity, no speeches). Proportions and placement are book decisions. A series continuation may sit inside the warm close, but do not add an ominous final hint merely because the long arc exists; an earlier, ordinary-seeming seed may be enough.

## The B-story pattern

A book-length secondary strand gives someone a concern beyond the investigation, develops through encounters and choices, and reaches a meaningful change. It may echo the main story without explaining the theme or cresting at exactly the same moment. Review a book that has no life beyond its case at outline stage; do not manufacture a late subplot to pass a generation gate.

A series-long investigation is not the same obligation. An early book may hold one unrecognised fact inside a worthwhile encounter and leave its significance unresolved. Do not force a revelation, ominous aside or personal connection merely to make the seed register as a subplot; track that seed and its eventual development in the series plan.

## Failure modes (name them in review)

Unearned gravity runs · premature moral closure · comedy that undercuts a beat the reader needed to keep · narrator-wit drift · the lecture-payoff (a character explains the theme) · slapstick · a lead whose voice disappears whenever the subject becomes serious. Preserve earned grief rather than forcing cheerfulness; continuous sombre delivery is not the only form of respect.

**Efficient but joyless:** active events, competent answers and clear transitions survive, while the invitations, playful resistance and familiar exchanges that made the company desirable disappear. Compare before and after; a clearer chapter may still have suffered this regression. Restore the relationship-specific exchange, not a scattering of new one-liners.

**Ambient wit:** every mouth polished, every exchange ending on somebody's crafted final word — one exceptionally clever conversationalist wearing the whole cast. The fix is that the lead owns the room and everyone else is vivid in their own currency. Distinguish what people want, notice, do and sound like rather than rationing who is allowed to be a forceful presence. Do not flatten a major character to protect the lead's brightness, or give everyone the lead's repartee to make them memorable. Competence remains local and consequential.

**The shrunken lead:** the opposite failure, and the one restraint rules cause. The lead's best lines have gone to the supporting cast or the narrator, the lead mostly loses, apologises or reacts, and readers say they would rather be someone else in the book. Restore the lead's spoken wit, wins and delight; do not "fix" it by dimming everyone else.
